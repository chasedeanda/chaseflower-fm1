# CORD 1.0.2

**Superseded by 1.0.3:** the Hub range checker identifies 936 as reserved by sloopDX. Use 1.0.3 or later (`FM-1_26730001`) for listing and hardware acceptance.

CORD now has its own firmware identity, `FM-1_936`, separate from CoralFlower’s `FM-1_930`. The package, running app and update loader agree (`ota-FM-1_936` while updating). The CORD installer verifies the selected package’s identity and continues to support earlier CORD/CoralFlower packages and backups.

Both Puya P25Q80 (`0x856014`) and newer UC25HQ80 (`0xB36014`) 1 MiB flash chips are accepted by the app and loader. Unknown chips remain RAM-only in the app and are blocked from erase/program operations in the loader. Flash layout and saved-data formats are unchanged.

Added Firmware Hub listing metadata, a real 240 × 240 CORD screenshot and a submission checklist. Hardware acceptance for this package is pending: these source, build and simulated-update checks do not establish successful V15/custom-firmware installation, switch-away or interrupted-update recovery on a physical FM-1.
