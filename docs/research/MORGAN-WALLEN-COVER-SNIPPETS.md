playback. The original chord durations, picking patterns, riffs and melody are
not encoded. Cover Me Up follows the compound 6/8 practice grid; the other seven
use 4/4. Choosing a phrase does not assert that later sections use its chords.

bass part. What I Want keeps its B Dorian setup and major IV chord, E;
it does not substitute natural minor's Em. Explicit chord buttons remain
performance overrides.
