# Play guitar for five minutes per Vibe

Use **1.0.3-dev.g1.1**. Start on **Jam**, the first screen after boot. Press **HOME** once for **Flow**. Turn **K1 left for Haze** or **right for Drive**, then press **PLAY**. K1–K4 are the four knobs left to right. From Flow, turn **ALGORITHM** one click left to get back to Jam.

| Time | What to do | What to watch or hear |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:45 | On Jam, play guitar over the first loop. | Chords and drums start together. The four beat squares step in time. The bar number goes from **16** back to **1** with no break in the music. |
| 0:45–1:30 | On Jam, sweep **K1 Energy** low to high, then **K3 Space** dry to wet. Turn **K2 Feel** near 0%, 35%, then 100%, waiting for each change. | Energy changes the band’s intensity. Space changes the effect amount. Feel shows **At bar …** first. When that bar arrives, the applied Feel line changes to **Held**, **Gated**, or **ARP**. |
| 1:30–2:10 | Press **ARP**. Turn **K1** to **On**, then **Off**, then **Auto**. Wait for each to land. With On, try **K4 Rate**. Press **HOME** to return to Jam. | With On you hear individual notes. Off plays the chord together. The ARP page says **Next chord** while the change waits. |
| 2:10–3:20 | Press **HOME** for Flow. Turn **K2 left** for Repeat, then **right** for Jam. Turn **K3** up to 100%. If Jam is blocked, use the Pins steps below. | Repeat plays the same part when the bar count rolls over. Jam can change Groove, Feel, or FX when it rolls over. The applied line shows what you’re hearing. |
| 3:20–4:30 | On Flow, turn **ALGORITHM left one click**. On Jam, turn **K4 one click right** for Fresh Take. After it lands, turn **K4 one click left** for Back One. | The screen says **At bar 1**. The progression title changes when that bar arrives, then returns after Back One. |
| 4:30–5:00 | Press **HOME** for Flow and turn **K1** toward the other Vibe. Let it switch while you keep playing. | The queued Vibe appears immediately. The applied Vibe changes at **bar 1** and the music keeps going. Check that the displayed key and BPM stay the same. |

Play the same pass with the other Vibe. If a change is still waiting at the end of a step, keep playing until it lands. Across both passes, try one extra Fresh Take. Press **PLAY** again to stop and listen for any stuck note.

**Pins.** On Flow, press **SEL** or turn **K4** to open Pins. Turn **K4** to highlight **Groove**. Press **SEL** until it says **Free**. Repeat for **FX** to let both change, and free **Sound** if you want Fresh Take to choose sounds. Press **HOME** to return to Flow. A **Pinned** setting stays as you set it. Turning Feel yourself pins it.

**Pick a progression yourself.** Turn the **SELECT knob** on Jam or Core to see a name under **Preview**. Press the **SEL button** to queue that choice. The playing title stays at the top until **bar 1**, when both screens show the new title. With no preview open, **SEL** opens Chord Lab. To browse the full fixed catalog, stop playback, hold **GLO**, then turn **SELECT**.
