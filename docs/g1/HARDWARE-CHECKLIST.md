# G0 + G1 hardware audition — five minutes per Vibe

Use local **1.0.3-dev.g1**. Run this pass for **Haze**, then for **Drive** (at least five minutes each). Play guitar throughout. Treat the times as guides and extend a pass when needed to hear the next bar 1. If a saved setup shows Sound, Groove or FX pins, use Flow K4 and SEL to unpin those categories for the palette audition. Keep your preferred key and tempo; a live Vibe switch should retain both. Log the firmware version, observations and any USB audio/MIDI capture. This pass is pending actual hardware testing.

| Time | Check |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:45 | Choose the Vibe and press PLAY. Confirm chord and pulse on bar 1, clear current chord/key and XX/16, and continuous 16→1. Compare the overall balance under guitar. |
| 0:45–1:30 | Sweep Energy and Space and hear a smooth response; Feel 0/35/100 must show the next chord onset and then play held/gated/eighth-note articulation. Try manual Play It On/Off/Auto and adjust mode/rate/gate/octave/swing. Check Live keys remains independent. |
| 1:30–2:30 | Set Repeat, then Jam at Surprise 100; compare two loops. Repeat keeps the notes and rhythm schedule. Jam can change one of groove/Feel/FX per cycle while retaining roots. Try Surprise 0. Use Fill, Drop, then Back; Drop should persist across bar 1. |
| 2:30–4:20 | Request Fresh Take at least three times total across the two passes; verify the title/ID change waits for bar 1. Back One restores the preceding roles and harmony. Pin a sound or groove and verify it stays yours. Three-chord ideas repeat the third slot; source slash bass is preserved when drawn. |
| 4:20–5:00 | Queue the other Vibe around bar 7. The current 16 bars finish in the old Vibe, then the new one starts without a gap, keeping key/BPM/ARP/pins. STOP and check for held-note, clock or USB glitches. |

Record hardware callback/CPU worst case, stack high-water and voice shedding from the existing diagnostics if available. Note whether the band is comfortable to play over, especially intro balance and the articulation/FX changes. The host demos and linked sizes cannot answer that. SAVE is a static Song snapshot; REC still owns a manual take.
