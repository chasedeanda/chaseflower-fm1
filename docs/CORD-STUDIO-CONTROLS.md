# CORD Studio controls

Turn **SELECT** on an overview to move between Studio, Sound, Record and Help. Turn **ALGORITHM** to browse the panels. Studio and Sound show two full-width units at a time. In Studio, the four amp heads sit above the outboard units. In Sound, the same amp heads sit above Sound Library and Mix. **SEQ** goes straight to the outboard units from the overview and advances through them while zoomed in.

A bright frame and an arrow mark the active unit. The fixed strip below the rack shows that unit’s K1–K4 labels and values. You can edit from the overview or press **SEL** to move the camera in. Turn a knob on a zoomed outboard unit for a brief close-up of that control. Press **SEL** to move back out, or **HOME** when **SEL** is labeled Apply. **SELECT** changes pages inside a zoomed unit that has multiple pages. The colored **KEYS** footer shows which musician the keyboard is playing.

## Studio

**PLAY** starts or stops the band. The header shows key, scale, meter, tempo, the current chord and a segmented output meter. The chord readout follows a held manual chord while you play one.

### Rack design

Use the cabinet for genre and the faceplate for unit identity. Dream Pop, Indie and Pop Punk use walnut. New Wave and Ambient Soundscape use anodized aluminum. Lo-Fi uses charcoal tolex. The finish follows the genre selector immediately. An in-flight genre change still takes effect musically at the next bar.

| Unit | Faceplate and control | Live display |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chords and Layer | Amp heads with stitched grille cloth | Playing style in Studio and preset in Sound |
| Bass and Drums | Amp heads with perforated metal grilles | Playing style in Studio and preset or kit in Sound |
| Harmony | Brushed nickel with black inset knobs | Key and beat |
| Shaping | Green anodized metal with a perforated grille and matte black knobs | Energy setting and output level |
| Genre | Blue enamel with two light stepped selectors | Selected genre and family |
| ARP | Purple anodized metal with machined silver knobs | Routing mode and played step |
| Progression | Graphite plate with stitched grille cloth and a two-position switch | Applied source and active chord slot |

Mount plates flush with a seam between them. Use 16 px Inter Tight for names and 12 px mixed-case control labels. Keep values at 14 px when they fit and give long program names the wide display. The small badge, vents and hardware marks belong in the close view. Use recessed dark displays with lit segments for actual values and activity. Knobs keep their horizontal positions and hardware style through the camera move.

The earlier study read as vintage because the cream needle meter, bright wood, brushed texture across the whole surface and dense all-caps legends all drew attention at once. The current pass uses the machined surfaces and compact controls of the TX–6, the status metering of the SSL Pure Drive Octo and the recessed display and perforated fascia of the Symphony I/O. [Teenage Engineering TX–6](https://teenage.engineering/products/tx-6), [SSL Pure Drive Octo](https://solidstatelogic.com/products/puredriveocto), [Apogee Symphony I/O Mk II](https://apogeedigital.com/pdf/Apogee-Symphony-IO-MkII-User-Guide-V2.0.pdf)

Bass Something 2 is the reference for keeping the knob inside the instrument as the camera moves. The user-supplied studio photos informed the flush mounting, varied finishes, and distinct control styles. [Bass Something 2](https://imijes.github.io/bass-something-2/)

### Genre

Highlight the Genre unit with **ALGORITHM** or **SEQ** and turn **K1** or **PRESETS** to choose a genre. **K2** selects a genre family. **PRESETS** also works from the Studio overview. Genre changes the harmonic and rhythmic vocabulary of the whole band, including the two playing styles available for each musician. A genre change queues a new take for the next bar during playback. Your current tempo continues while the band plays. Open **Sound → Sound Library** in Genre mode and press **SEL** to load the curated palette, or press **EDIT** on the Sound overview to cycle through its alternatives.

| Genre | Playing character |
| --- | --- |
| Dream Pop | Sustained or pulsing chords, melodic bass, a soft beat and sparse upper figures |
| Indie | Chopped or ringing chords, offbeat bass, room drums and a chime line |
| New Wave | Short chord stabs, octave bass, a machine beat and stepped synth figures |
| Pop Punk | Palm-muted attacks, open chords, bass riffs locked to the kick, half-time and straight grooves, and a feedback-like Layer |
| Lo-Fi | Soft keys, a late bass pocket, half-time drums and short melodic fragments |
| Ambient Soundscape | Slow harmony, pedal bass, sparse percussion and evolving swells |

### Harmony and Shaping

Highlight **Harmony** with **ALGORITHM** or **SEQ** to change key, tempo, scale, or meter. Highlight **Shaping** for Space, Tempo, Energy, and Evolve. Harmony is also available as a named page within a zoomed Studio musician by turning **SELECT**.

| Page | Knob | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Harmony | K1 Key | Transposes the band to a new tonic. During playback the change lands at the next chord. |
| Harmony | K2 Tempo | Adjusts the internal tempo. When following an external MIDI clock, the source controls the tempo. |
| Harmony | K3 Scale | Changes the scale used by harmony and the generator. During playback it lands at the next chord. |
| Harmony | K4 Meter | Chooses 3/4, 4/4 or 6/8. During playback it lands at the next bar. |
| Shaping | K1 Space | Scales the audible chorus, delay and reverb together. Raise the relevant send on Sound → Sends if a part is dry. |
| Shaping | K2 Tempo | The same tempo control as on Harmony. |
| Shaping | K3 Energy | Raises or lowers the effective Activity of all four musicians around their current settings. 50% is neutral. Phrases gain or shed events and can change accents within the current take. |
| Shaping | K4 Evolve | Sets how often one musician automatically varies its phrase. 0% turns it off. 1–49% gives the band one change every 8 bars, 50–79% every 4 bars and 80–100% every 2 bars. Ambient doubles those intervals. Muted musicians and musicians with Activity below 35% are skipped. |

Press **EDIT** on the Studio overview to generate a new band take for the next bar. Zoom into a musician and press **EDIT** to regenerate only that part on the next sixteenth-note grid point, or eighth-note grid point in 6/8. EDIT also rotates eligible sounds in the genre palette. Pitched patches switch at the musical boundary and drum kits switch at the next bar. Saved user patches, edited patches and sounds you are holding stay selected.

### Custom progression

Highlight **Progression** and press **SEL** to open it. Press **EDIT** to open your draft. The first draft starts with a one-bar tonic chord. In the editor, **ALGORITHM** picks a chord slot and **SELECT** changes pages. The chord page edits degree, quality, color and length. The other pages expose timing, inversion, bass and phrase actions. On **Phrase**, choose Insert or Duplicate with **K1** and press **SEL** to add a slot.

Press **PLAY** in the editor to use the draft for all four musicians. If the band is already playing, the new progression starts at the next bar. The band keeps the draft’s chords, durations and meter when you regenerate a part. Drafts support 3/4, 4/4 and 6/8 in Studio and checkpoint using the existing draft storage when the unit is idle. To switch sources, open the **Progression** faceplate, turn **K1** to Bank or My draft, and press **SEL**.

### Musician panel

| Knob | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| K1 Style | Chooses between two ways of playing for the selected musician and genre. For example, Indie Bass offers Offbeat and Answer. |
| K2 Activity | Sets how busy that musician plays. The effect depends on the style and can include extra notes, accents and percussion hits. |
| K3 Level | Sets the musician’s mix level. This is the same level on Sound → Tone and in the GLO mixer. |
| K4 Sustain on Chords | Sets the envelope level while a chord is held. Sound → Envelope → Release sets how long it rings after note-off. |
| K4 Touch on Bass and Layer | Sets the velocity of generated notes. 50% leaves the programmed velocity as played. |
| K4 Punch on Drums | Sets the velocity of generated drum hits. |

Style and Activity changes take effect on the next playable grid point. Continuous knobs take single steps when turned slowly and five-unit steps when turned quickly.

### GLO mixer

Press **GLO** to open or close the mixer. K1–K4 set the levels of Chords, Bass, Drums and Layer in that order. Hold upper keys 1–4 for momentary mute or 5–8 for momentary solo. **EDIT** latches mute and **SEL** latches solo on the selected musician. On the mixer or zoomed Studio Drums panel, turn **PRESETS** left for a fill or right to drop or return the drums at the next bar. **SAVE** from the mixer opens Global settings.

## Sound

Open **Sound Library** at the end of the Sound rack. **K1 Library** selects the short Genre list or All sounds. Highlight a musician and use **K1 Preset** to browse its tones or kits. With Genre selected, press **SEL** to load the complete palette. Press **EDIT** on the Sound overview or in Sound Library to choose the next tone or kit for each musician. In a musician’s Tone panel, **EDIT** changes that musician only. While playing, the pitched sounds change immediately and the drum kit changes at the next bar. The palette keeps the current levels, pan, sends and shared effects settings.

A highlighted Sound musician shows the Tone controls. Zoom in and turn **SELECT** to reach the other named pages. The page counter shows where you are.

| Page | K1 | K2 | K3 | K4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tone | Preset or drum kit | Mix level | First synth macro, such as filter cutoff on VA tones | Pan |
| Envelope | Attack, the time to reach full level | Decay, the time to fall toward Sustain | Sustain, the held level | Release, the time to fade after note-off |
| Sends | Drive amount | Chorus send | Delay send | Reverb send |
| FX Presets | Browse shared FX presets | | | |

Drums has Tone, Sends and FX Presets. **PRESETS** also browses the sound or kit. Sends shows a preview of the selected musician’s drive curve, chorus sweep, delay taps and reverb tail. Each graph responds to that musician’s knob. From the Sound overview, press **FX** to jump to FX Presets. Turn **K1** or **PRESETS** to highlight a preset and press **SEL** to apply it to the band. Use **SELECT** to turn to another Sound page. **SAVE** opens preset management, and pressing **EDIT** or **FX** opens the EQ and effects editor for the loaded preset. **LFO** from a zoomed pitched musician opens its detailed synth editor.

## Arpeggiator

Select Chords or Layer, highlight the ARP unit with **SEQ**, and press **SEL** to zoom in. The **ARP** button opens the same panel directly. On the Pattern page, **K1 Arp For** chooses Off, Keys or Band. Keys arpeggiates the notes you hold. Band arpeggiates that musician’s generated chord tones with the transport running. **K2 Mode** selects the note order or strum mode, **K3 Octave** sets the range, and **K4 Rate** sets the clock division or the spacing in milliseconds for a strum.

Turn **SELECT** to the timing page. **K1 Gate** sets how much of each step a note occupies, **K2 Swing** shifts alternating steps, **K3 Latch** keeps the arpeggio going after key release, and **K4 Glide** sets the Chords glide time.

## Record and Help

**Record** shows the four generated phrases and the playhead. **SEL** opens a two-bar view of the highlighted musician. **REC** starts or finishes a manual take of up to eight bars. Highlight **Saved Takes** at the end of the rack, turn **K1** to select one of the three slots, and hold **SAVE** for three seconds to clear it. Release early to cancel. Finish recording or stop saved-take playback before clearing. Use USB audio capture to record the full generated mix as audio.

**Help** contains Start here, Harmony, Playing and Capture. Turn **ALGORITHM** to select a topic and **SEL** to open it. The Harmony lesson shows the role of the note you play. **K1** switches between Auto and Manual harmony. **EDIT** opens Chord Lab.
